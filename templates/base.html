Your privacy is our priority. This policy explains what data EMS collects, how it's used, and how it's protected within your organisation.
👤
Employee Data
Names, contact info, and payroll stored securely and used only for HR operations.
📅
Attendance Records
Daily check-in/out logs used exclusively for payroll computation.
💰
Salary & Advances
Financial data is encrypted at rest and accessible only to account owners.
🔐
Authentication
Session tokens expire automatically. No persistent tracking cookies are used.
Data We Collect
We collect only the minimum information required to operate the system:
  • Identity Employee names, phone numbers, age, and gender.
  • Attendance Daily presence/absence records and dates.
  • Payroll Monthly salary, advance payments, and payment status.
  • Account Username and hashed password for secure authentication.
We do not collect biometric data, location data, or device identifiers.
How We Use Your Data
All collected data is used exclusively to operate the Employee Management System:
  • Computing pro-rated salary based on attendance records.
  • Tracking advance payments and remaining balances.
  • Generating CSV payroll and attendance reports on request.
  • Authenticating users and maintaining secure sessions.
Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
Data Retention
Data is retained for as long as your account is active. When you delete an employee, all associated attendance and salary records are permanently removed from the database immediately. Account deletion removes all stored data with no recovery.
Sessions & Cookies
EMS uses a single session cookie to maintain your login state. This cookie:
  • Is HTTP-only (not accessible by JavaScript).
  • Is cleared when you log out or close the browser.
  • Contains no personal data — only an encrypted session identifier.
No third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies are used.

Data compliance practices built into EMS to protect employee information and meet professional HR standards.
🔒
Password Hashing
All passwords hashed with SHA-256 before storage. Plain-text passwords are never stored.
🧱
Rate Limiting
Login endpoints limited to 10 requests/min. All routes protected against abuse.
👁️
Access Control
Each user can only access their own data. Cross-account data access is prevented.
🗑️
Right to Erasure
Deleting an employee removes all their records cascade — no orphaned data left behind.
Data Handling Standards
EMS follows these data handling principles:
  • Minimisation Only essential data is collected — no extras.
  • Purpose Limitation Data is used strictly for HR and payroll functions.
  • Integrity All writes validated server-side before being committed to the database.
  • Transparency This panel documents every data practice in plain language.
Employee Data Rights
As an account owner, you are the data controller for your employees' information. Your responsibilities:
  • Inform employees that their data is being processed in this system.
  • Obtain appropriate consent before entering personal details.
  • Delete records when an employee's relationship ends.
  • Not retain data beyond its legitimate HR purpose.
Export & Portability
All your data can be exported at any time via the CSV Export feature. Exports include employee records, attendance logs, and salary details in a standard open format — ensuring you are never locked in.

Security is built-in, not bolted on. EMS applies multiple layers of protection to keep your HR data safe.
🛡️
Brute-Force Protection
Login limited to 10 attempts/min per IP. Repeated failures are automatically blocked.
🔑
Hashed Credentials
Passwords stored as SHA-256 hashes. Your real password is never saved anywhere.
🚦
Rate Limiting
All 20+ routes are individually rate-limited to prevent abuse and DoS attacks.
🗄️
Isolated Data
Every query is user-scoped. It is impossible to access another user's employee data.
Security Layers
  • Layer 1 Authentication — Session-based login required for all protected routes.
  • Layer 2 Authorisation — Every DB query filters by user_id from the session.
  • Layer 3 Rate Limiting — Flask-Limiter enforces per-IP request caps on all endpoints.
  • Layer 4 Input Validation — All form inputs are validated and sanitised server-side.
  • Layer 5 Cascade Deletes — FK constraints prevent orphaned data on employee removal.
Your Account Security Tips
Keep your account secure by following these best practices:
  • Use a strong, unique password (12+ characters, mixed case, numbers).
  • Never share your login credentials with anyone, even colleagues.
  • Always Log out when using a shared or public device.
  • Check the Debugger PIN is only known to you in development mode.
  • In production, set a strong SECRET_KEY environment variable.
Reporting Security Issues
If you discover a security vulnerability in EMS:
  • Do not exploit or publicly disclose it before informing the maintainer.
  • Report it privately with a clear description of the issue and reproduction steps.
  • Responsible disclosure is appreciated and will be acknowledged.
